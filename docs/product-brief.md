# FrontDeskWise product brief

## Promise

FrontDeskWise is the AI front desk for local businesses: it answers customer questions, captures intent, and helps visitors take the next business action.

## Primary user

An owner or operator of a local service business who loses opportunities because the website is hard to navigate, the team is busy, or questions arrive after hours.

## v1 product surfaces

- Public URL-to-demo onboarding.
- Tenant workspace and business profile.
- Website sources and approved answers.
- Grounded website chat widget.
- Lead capture and booking/call actions.
- Conversation history and unanswered-question queue.
- Owner dashboard with revenue-oriented metrics.
- Agent and widget customization.

## Trust rules

- Never retrieve across tenants.
- Every answer must carry source context internally.
- If confidence is low, say so and offer human follow-up.
- Actions require explicit configuration and an auditable event.
- Public demos are clearly labeled as sandbox previews.
